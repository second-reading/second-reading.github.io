Privacy

Short, because there isn't much to say. Last updated 10 November 2026.

Who we are

Second Reading Games is a small independent games studio in the UK. For anything about your data, email hello@secondreading.games.

The mailing list

If you give us your email address in a sign-up form on this site, we use it to email you when a game is released, and now and then if we have other news. That's all. We don't sell it, share it or use it for anything else.

We keep it until you ask us to take you off the list. To do that, reply to any of our emails or write to hello@secondreading.games, and we'll delete your address.

We rely on your consent to hold it, and you can withdraw that whenever you like.

The form is sent through FormSubmit, a service that passes it on to our inbox by email. FormSubmit is based outside the UK, so your address passes through their servers on its way to us; their own privacy policy covers how they handle it.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you (at most, your email address and when you signed up), to correct it, or to delete it. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

The rest of the site

No cookies, no analytics, no tracking, no adverts. The fonts are served from this site, not from anyone else's.

The games here save your progress and settings in your own browser (its local storage). That stays on your device and is never sent to us; clearing your browser's site data removes it.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses to keep the service running and secure; see GitHub's privacy statement. We don't see those logs.
